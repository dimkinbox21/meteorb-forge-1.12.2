ChestDump ported to 1.12.2, dumps the chest on its key

Confirmed in game: one key press moves the 27 inventory slots into an open
chest and leaves the hotbar, which is the whole point of the module.

Three 1.12.2 changes the port had to absorb, each measured rather than guessed:

  - the click is a ClickType enum, not an int, and its constants did not
    survive MCP - ClickType.QUICK_MOVE does not compile, so the shift-click
    is ClickType.values()[1], which the mapping confirms as QUICK_MOVE;
  - ItemStack.stackSize is private, so the count read is getCount();
  - GuiScreenEvent.KeyboardInputEvent exists in this Forge but nothing posts
    it - no class in the Forge jar refers to it and the released
    GuiScreen.handleKeyboardInput carries no Forge call - so an event
    subscription there would never fire. The key still goes through the
    coremod transformer, and both of that hook's 1.8 fixes came with it:
    Escape is never swallowed, and the suppress value is the key itself
    rather than 1, which in 1.8 made any key close any screen.

The jar manifest no longer sets FMLCorePluginContainsFMLMod: Forge 14 warns
against a coremod and a mod sharing one jar, and it is not needed here.
